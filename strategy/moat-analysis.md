# Competitive Moat Analysis

## 1. Local-First Resilience
Most AI competitors require 100% connectivity. EasyTutor's local Ollama fallback makes it superior in emerging markets and high-privacy environments.

## 2. Multi-Portal Domain Depth
By isolating portals (High School, University), we provide syllabus-aware intelligence that generic AI tutors cannot match.

## 3. Hybrid Routing Efficiency
Lowering operational costs by routing foundations to local hardware while reserving cloud intelligence for "Mastery > 70%" scenarios.
